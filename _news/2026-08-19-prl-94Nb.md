New paper in [*Physical Review Letters*](https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/538w-rhqb) — the n_TOF Collaboration's first measurement of the <sup>94</sup>Nb(n,&gamma;) cross section brings AGB models into agreement with the <sup>94</sup>Mo excess in presolar SiC grains, resolving a two-decade-old discrepancy.
